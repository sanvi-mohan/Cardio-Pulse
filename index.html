OUR MAIN INNOVATION

Your Smartwatch.
Your Personal Health Companion.

Cardio Pulse aims to bring wearable technology and personal health monitoring together. Compatible smartwatches can provide supported measurements without requiring users to enter every reading manually.

  • Wearable device connectivity
  • Automatic data synchronization
  • Manual measurement backup
  • Health history and trends
ABOUT CARDIO PULSE

Health monitoring helps individuals understand changes in their body by observing important health measurements, physical activity and lifestyle patterns. Cardio Pulse brings this information together in a single platform.
